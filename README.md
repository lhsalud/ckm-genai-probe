As part of ongoing doctoral research into clinical feature engineering and the development of non-
invasive digital health platforms, I am currently in an initial, “proof-of-concept”, phase of building
computable clinical decision support (CDS) triage rules. These rules specifically focus on a small subset of
Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) conditions, managing complex interactions between patient
symptoms (e.g., sick days, paresthesia), lab values (e.g., eGFR, hyperkalemia), and specific medication
classes (e.g., SGLT2 inhibitors, MRAs, RASi). The goal is to translate these targeted clinical presentations
into standardized triage levels (URGENT, ROUTINE, EMERGENT, SELF CARE). However, before these rules
can be encoded into executable clinical logic (such as CQL for a CQF Ruler engine), their underlying logic
must be anchored to validated clinical evidence and guidelines (e.g., KDIGO, ADA, ACC/AHA).
